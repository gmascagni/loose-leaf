Specialty Green Tea (Dragonwell / Sencha)

Delicate low-temp steeping to preserve fresh umami, sweet grassy aromas, and high L-theanine amino acids without bitter tannins.

Ratio 1:50 Temp: 172°F (78°C) Leaf Style: Whole Leaf (Steamed / Pan-Fired)

Steeping Parameters & Phases

Phase 1: Vessel Preheat 15s

Warm teapot or glass with warm water, then discard water.

Phase 2: 1st Steeping Infusion 120s

Pour 78°C water gently over leaves. Do not agitate.

Phase 3: Decant & 2nd Infusion Prep 90s

Pour completely into server. Leaves ready for 2nd steep.
