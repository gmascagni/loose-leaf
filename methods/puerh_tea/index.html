Aged Pu-erh Tea (Shou / Sheng Gongfu)

Post-fermented tea cakes from Yunnan. Dense liquor with sweet damp earth, camphor wood, and dark chocolate undertones.

Ratio 1:20 Temp: 210°F (99°C) Leaf Style: Aged Compressed Tea Leaves & Gushu Buds

Steeping Parameters & Phases

Phase 1: Awakening Boiling Rinse 10s

Pour boiling water over leaf cake chunks; flash discard after 10s to awaken aged leaves.

Phase 2: 1st Deep Earth Infusion 25s

Steep 25 seconds with active rolling boil (99°C).

Phase 3: Decant & Subsequent Steeps 20s

Decant completely into pitcher. Leaves endure 8-12 successive infusions.
