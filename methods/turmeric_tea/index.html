Golden Turmeric Botanical Tea (Herbal Infusion)

Caffeine-free wellness infusion combining golden turmeric root, ginger, lemongrass, black pepper (for curcumin absorption), and raw honey.

Ratio 1:40 Temp: 208°F (98°C) Leaf Style: Crushed Organic Turmeric Root & Botanicals

Steeping Parameters & Phases

Phase 1: Botanical Hydration 15s

Place crushed turmeric and botanicals into infuser.

Phase 2: Deep Botanical Steep 300s

Steep 5 minutes with boiling 98°C water for rich golden extraction.

Phase 3: Honey & Citrus Stir 30s

Stir in raw honey and squeeze of fresh lemon.
