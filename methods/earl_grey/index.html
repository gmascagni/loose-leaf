Earl Grey Tea (Italian Bergamot Oil Infused)

Aromatic black tea infused with natural cold-pressed oil of Italian bergamot citrus. Fragrant floral citrus aroma balanced with malty cocoa depth.

Ratio 1:50 Temp: 203°F (95°C) Leaf Style: Full-Leaf Black & Cold-Pressed Bergamot Oil

Steeping Parameters & Phases

Phase 1: Vessel Warm 15s

Rinse ceramic teapot with hot water.

Phase 2: Aromatic Citrus Steep 210s

Steep untouched at 95°C for 3.5 minutes to release citrus aromatics.

Phase 3: Decant 30s

Separate tea leaves from liqueur.
