Himalayan Darjeeling Tea (The Champagne of Teas)

Grown in high-altitude misty Himalayan ridges. Renowned for delicate muscatel grape clarity, peach notes, and crisp amber finish.

Ratio 1:50 Temp: 190°F (88°C) Leaf Style: FTGFOP1 Whole Leaf

Steeping Parameters & Phases

Phase 1: Vessel Preheat 15s

Rinse porcelain or glass teapot with warm water to maintain steep temperature.

Phase 2: Aromatic Himalayan Steep 180s

Pour 88°C water over leaves. Steep 3 minutes for peak muscatel grape bouquet.

Phase 3: Decant & Serve 30s

Strain completely into teacup to stop extraction.
